Meet La Cosecha

Our mission

Equitable healthcare starts with showing up.

The mission of La Cosecha is to increase allies fighting for equitable healthcare for farmworkers by raising awareness among future healthcare providers about the health challenges farmworkers face, while working to expand access to preventive healthcare resources for farmworking communities surrounding greater Los Angeles.

01

Raise awareness

Help future healthcare providers understand the health challenges experienced by farmworkers.

02

Expand access

Support access to preventive healthcare resources in the communities that sustain us.

03

Build community

Bring students together around service and advocacy for equitable healthcare.

Our founders

Rooted in lived experience.

Denise Jiménez and Erika Escalera founded La Cosecha while studying medicine at UCLA and are now family medicine residents. Both come from migrant families whose parents worked in the fields. Drawing on their families’ experiences, they created La Cosecha to raise awareness among future physicians of the unique healthcare needs of migrant Indigenous communities.
